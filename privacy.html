Privacy Policy — ARC Ops for Jira

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Contact: arc.agent.ops@gmail.com

ARC Ops for Jira is a free Forge app that turns a Jira ticket into a plan: it shows a rule-based triage panel on a Jira issue and, at your choice, publishes a decision brief to a Confluence space. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

The app only accesses data inside the Atlassian site where it is installed, using the scopes you consent to at install time:

  • Jira issue data (title, description, issue type, priority, status, and links) — to render the triage panel.
  • Your Atlassian account information — to identify you inside the app.
  • Confluence spaces — to list the spaces you can publish a decision brief to.
  • Confluence page creation — only when you click “Publish decision brief.” The app never creates a page on its own.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to render the triage panel and, when you ask it to, publish a decision brief to the Confluence space you select. The triage is rule-based and computed at request time inside the Forge function. The app makes no external AI calls and sends your data to no external service.

Storage

The app does not store your Jira or Confluence data outside Atlassian. Decision briefs you publish live in your Confluence space until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its access to your site.

Sharing, analytics, and tracking

We do not share your data with any third party. The app contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Security

The app runs on Atlassian’s Forge platform. Access is governed by the scopes you consent to when installing, and the app can only do what those scopes allow.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

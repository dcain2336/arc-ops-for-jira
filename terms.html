ARC Ops for Jira is a free Forge app that shows a rule-based triage panel on a Jira issue and, at your choice, publishes a decision brief to a Confluence space. By installing or using the app, you agree to these terms.

What the app does

The app reads Jira issue data to render triage (classification, confidence, try-first steps, blockers, and decision options). It never modifies your Jira issues. It creates a Confluence page only when you click “Publish decision brief,” and only in the space you select. You are responsible for the content of any brief you publish.
